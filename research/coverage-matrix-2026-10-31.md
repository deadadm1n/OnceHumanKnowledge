## Standard for marking a system 'verified complete'
- List of affected objects/abilities with **current** names.
- One directly cited source for each important mechanic; source and patch date.
- Contradictions resolved by later patch and applicable scenario.
- Quantitative formulas either measured and repeatable or explicitly unknown.
- Examples demonstrate a *legal* loadout or reproducible production process.
- Machine-readable index and human navigation are updated.

As of this audit, **no system meets this exhaustive standard**. The reference is useful but is still expanding.
