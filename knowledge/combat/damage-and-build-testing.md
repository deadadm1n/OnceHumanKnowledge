# Damage types, legal builds, buffs and defensible DPS experiments

**Scope:** modern combat; assumes Isles of Abyss unless stated. **Never assume a universal formula.**

## Damage taxonomy
Track separately:
- Attack/base weapon parameters, weapon damage percentage, final damage modifiers.
- Normal bullet hits vs additional weapon procs.
- Critical rate/chance and critical damage.
- Weakspot damage and the **actual percentage of hits landing weakspots**.
- Weapon-effect specific Shrapnel, Fast Gunner, Frost Vortex, Bounce, Bull's Eye, and Unstable Bomber.
- Status damage, elemental bonuses, weapon vs status vulnerability, defensive mitigation.
- Reload, ammunition effects, fire rate, timing windows, range and boss phase invulnerability.

A status proc that can crit does **not** automatically share every bullet-damage modifier. February 2026 update explicitly distinguished vulnerability categories.

## Build legality
- Six gear slots: Head, Mask, Chest/Jacket, Gloves, Pants, Shoes.
- **Only one Key Armor piece per build** — independently corroborated in community references; in-game equip behavior remains decisive.
- Four owned Lonewolf pieces can provide four-piece set bonus without replacing any: Hood + Mask + Jacket + Gloves. Then Pants and Shoes are free for Bastille / one key armor.
- Example *hypothesis*: four Lonewolf + Bastille Pants + Earthly Boots + 6★ Memento + Bull's Eye-marking sniper. This is a legal slot arrangement but its DPS advantage **has not been proven**.
- Switching to Beret (Head) removes Lonewolf Hood and therefore the 4-piece bonus. Compare losses as well as gains.

## Minimum repeatable trial
Record: patch, scenario, Tech, target health and defenses, distance, shot count, critical and weakspot rate, weapon tier/stars, active mod attributes, calibration blueprint, ammo, food buff, Deviation effect, starting Power, 10s/30s damage, reloads and any missed shots.
- Run minimum 3 repetitions per setup.
- Separate 10-second burst, 30-second sustained and boss phase time-to-kill.
- Compare one changed variable at a time; quantify uncertainty.
- If enemy is invulnerable or weakspot-hidden during test, exclude or annotate that time explicitly.

## What cannot be inferred
- A 20k weakspot screenshot cannot establish 20k DPS.
- Weapon tooltip base damage alone cannot establish overall ranking.
- A secondary Bull's Eye mark may not remain through swaps: verify the actual status icon/timer.
- Blueprint star comparison is confounded when two weapons have very different investment.
