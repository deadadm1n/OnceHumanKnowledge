## Benchmark protocol
Record: scenario + game version + Tech tier, enemy + difficulty, actual equipment and stars, calibration blueprint, mod levels/attributes, ammo, buffs, secondary mark, firing range, weakspot hit percentage, test duration and initial/final damage.
Measure (1) single hit, (2) 10-second burst, (3) 30-second sustained, (4) full-magazine DPS, (5) reload downtime. Repeat each trial >=3 times and report variability. Never compare a 6★/max-Tech weapon to 1★/low-Tech gear without noting unequal investment.

## No unverified formulas
- '20,000 weakspot damage' is a single observed number, not DPS.
- Additional DMG vs Weapon DMG vs Attack vs weakspot multipliers must not be assumed additive/multiplicative without tests.
- Key Armor exclusivity, Bull's Eye mark transfer, Shrapnel hit parts and crit interactions are **pending direct test**.
