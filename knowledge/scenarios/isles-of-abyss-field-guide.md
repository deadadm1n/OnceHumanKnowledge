# Isles of Abyss: survival, Tech, ocean and progression field guide

**Scope:** Isles of Abyss PvE, launched October 21, 2026. **Checked:** October 31, 2026. **Confidence:** OFFICIAL for statements below; extrapolations explicitly labeled.

## Progression — what carries over?
- Character begins at maximum level in this scenario, but its own **Tech tree** gates crafting capability.
- Your existing blueprint and mod unlocks transfer, **not necessarily their complete active capabilities**. Craftable equipment rarity and the count of active mod attributes rise with Tech.
- **Cradle is disabled** in this scenario. Generic Manibus or Way of Winter Cradle guides are inapplicable.
- Heart of the Ocean comes from challenges, objectives, exploration and Voyager activity, and unlocks certain challenge rewards. It is a reward key, not an interchangeable universal currency.
- Phase and world rules should be checked against the live scenario screen; don't generalize a server's current phase.

## Water hazards, solutions and equipment
| Environmental problem | Officially described countermeasure | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Ocean contamination | Basic Diving Suit; Shark Diving Suit has stronger protection and swim-speed increase | Tech-gated availability |
| Cold and contamination during diving | Fish Oil Paste | Temporary effect |
| Oxygen depleted at depth | Oxygen Tank activates automatically | Consumable logistics need testing |
| Need emergency ascent | Inflatable Float | Don't assume infinite uses |
| Need surface footing | Air Cushion | Temporary pad |
| Underwater enemies | Spear Gun | Damage effectiveness target-dependent |
| Mist of Deceit | Mist Core Clarity Soup and Blaze Core Lantern | Lantern consumes durability |
| Black Tide | Black Tide Protective Mask | Consumes durability, slows Sanity loss |
| Time Rain | Time-Reverse Cookies or Time-Reverse Raincoat | Raincoat reduces movement speed and consumes durability |

## Ship systems
- Ocean Tech unlocks additional ship cannons with different firing modes; some projectile cannons include bullet-drop pre-aim lines.
- Ram and Naval Mine Launcher create different engagement patterns: direct collision versus trap placement.
- Wheel selection can change active ship cannon.
- Ship with Trawl Net has a *chance* to collect fish while sailing; Advanced Trawl also salvages surface supplies. Abundant Fishing Talisman boosts trawl-net fishing efficiency. **No verified catch-per-minute constant.**
- Tidal Cage enables fish breeding; Advanced Tidal Cage shortens breeding cycles.
- Drilling Well extracts abyssal sea oil; Black Tide Fuel can raise generator electricity cap and propeller speed cap under supported facilities.
- Automatic collection of specialty resources is constrained by **the island range of the matching calamity zone** (October 29 hotfix). Offshore location alone is not enough.

## Boss and enemy mechanics (official)
- **Sunken Lord:** server purification reaching 100% opens platform; Calamity Hatchling + entrance battle precede inner boss. Encounter includes interacting tentacles, barrages, slams, slime and illusions. This is a coordination challenge, not simply a stationary DPS dummy.
- **Drifting Greenhouse:** Phase II island encounter; poisonous pools and plant rewards after victory.
- **Abyssal Iron Guard:** steam shield protects allies; prioritize this unit to reduce enemy shielding.
- **Drowning Light Apostle:** lamp state increases both damage dealt and received. Potential burst window; verify exact timing.
- **Abyssal Sea Kins:** death barnacle explosion can heal nearby deviants. Separate targets or control kill timing.

## Economic dependencies
`Tech -> rarity + active mods -> personal DPS`  
`Tech -> cannons + extraction -> ship viability`  
`ocean exploration -> Heart of the Ocean -> challenge mod rewards`  
`ship travel + trawl -> seafood + surface supplies`

## Tests (do not confuse theory with evidence)
- Same gear, before and after each Tech unlock: note craft quality and actually active mod lines.
- 10-minute trawl routes with/without talisman; document wind/current, sailing speed and fish totals.
- Measure how much Black Tide Fuel increases power/speed and duration.
- Record Sunken Lord attacks, weakspots and immunity intervals before rating weapon DPS.
