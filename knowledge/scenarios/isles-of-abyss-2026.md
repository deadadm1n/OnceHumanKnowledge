# Isles of Abyss (PvE) — verified October 2026 baseline

**Version:** 3.1.1 (2026-10-21). **Source precedence:** 2026-10-21 official update > 2026-10-16 developer preview > independent 2026-10-24 guide.

## Rules [OFFICIAL]
- PvE, approximately 21-day main cycle, Phase I approximately 5–10 days depending on purification progress, Phase II 11 days, then rest stage.
- 12 worlds per server, no launch Vision Tag; future Vision World is separate.
- Endgame Sunken Lord unlocks based on server purification progress.
- Existing blueprints and mods carry over, but equipment craftable rarity and count of active mod attributes are constrained by **Tech progression**.
- Heart of the Ocean is gained from scenario challenges, objectives, treasures and Voyager gameplay; used for challenge reward collection.
- Two new armor mods are Precision Load (lower body; weakspot hits at long range recover bullets) and Turret Enhancement (gloves; increases damage dealt by used facilities). Obtain from Sunken Lord or Heart of the Ocean reward consumption in the specified challenges.

## Scenario-specific combat [SECONDARY, NEEDS IN-GAME CONFIRMATION]
- Updated 2026-10-24 Mobalytics guide reports **Cradle unavailable in Isles of Abyss**. Do not suggest Cradle Override builds for this scenario without verifying any later patch changes.

## Sea, biomes and anomalies [OFFICIAL]
- Haywire Zones include Mist of Deceit, Black Tide, Time Rain and Skyfire; effects can alter vision, temperature, sanity and supply consumption.
- Calamity Tower progresses from solo story challenges to group fights and a Phase II abyss challenge; ships/cannons and maneuvering are relevant.
- Oyster opening has a *chance* to yield Shells, Conches, Shellfish Meat, Seaweed and Pearls. Conches and shells are inputs for certain facilities. No guaranteed conch drop rate has been published in the source.
- Tidal Cage permits fish breeding; Advanced Tidal Cage has a shorter breeding cycle.
- Ships are movable territory. Save a design layout; do not assume ordinary land territory limits apply to ship facilities.

## New entities [OFFICIAL]
- Duet: chance from Sunken Lord; changes territory weather with effects on player/facilities.
- Chaos Rebecca: obtainable through Chaos Tower.
- Morphed deviations: Shattered Maiden Sailor Suit (Calamity Tower), Director Fox Vacation (Drifting Greenhouse), Gingerbread House Oceanic Cottage (Calamity Tower), Whalepup Don't Get Wet (Voyagers), Space Turner Water Cube (Sea Calamity Zone), Snow Globe Sunken Treasure (Calamity Nest), Employee No. H37 Submarine Attitude (Voyagers).
- Cosmic Visitor and Snowline Elite Vest are new Frost-related blueprints from the October 21 launch gifts. Pairing is intended thematically, not independently proven best-in-slot.
