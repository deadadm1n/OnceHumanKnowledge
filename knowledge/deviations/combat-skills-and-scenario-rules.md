# Combat Deviation mechanics — updated December 2025 and scenario distinctions

## December 23, 2025 overhaul (OFFICIAL)
- Synchronized Combat Deviations gained **two active skills**, Battle Skill (cooldown) and Ultimate (power-dependent).
- Ultimates consume Deviation energy; specific effects differ by Deviation.
- Can equip/switch synced Combat Deviations in gear backpack after update; **switching clears Deviation Power and resets battle-skill cooldown**.
- Some ultimates offer summons, cover, crowd control or direct attacks. There are wheel mode and alternate tap-to-deploy control schemes.
- This update applied to all scenarios **except Deviation: Survive, Capture, Preserve** (RaidZone has differences).
Source: https://www.oncehuman.game/m/news/devBlog/20251218/40781_1277420.html

## Deviation: Survive, Capture, Preserve is NOT the baseline for Isles of Abyss
- This standalone scenario has Symbiosis progression and three-Deviation squads.
- Symbiosis level affects captured Deviation level and ability strength.
- Official targeting priority after October 2025: taunting > marked > active player target > attackers > aggro.
- Combat has its own real-time PvP and PvE Deviation arenas.
Source: https://www.oncehuman.game/news/devBlog/20251024/40781_1266818.html

## Practical implications
- Changing combat Deviations right before a boss can wipe accumulated Power, so a rapid-swap strategy may not work.
- Guide claims that every scenario allows a three-Deviation combat squad are false: the official three-Deviation team rule belongs to the dedicated scenario.
- Test whether particular statuses and mark buffs apply to Deviation ultimates separately from ordinary bullet damage.
- Check actual current ability text; old guides focused on simple deploy/recall miss the added Battle Skill.
