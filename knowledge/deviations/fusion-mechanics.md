# Deviation Fusion mechanics — evidence audit (2026-10-31)

## Primary rules (OFFICIAL, September 2025)
- Two parent Deviations are selected. The resulting Deviation randomly inherits type and traits from parent candidates.
- **Parent Deviations are NOT consumed.** Official launch rules specified each parent could be used as a fusion parent once.
- **Extra fusion materials ARE consumed**, including Deviations, furniture and compatible animals.
- Materials influence fusion outcome probabilities and can add possible traits.
- The fusion system was rolled out beyond the dedicated Deviation scenario, but the latter uses its own battle rules.
Source: https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20250919/40780_1260194.html

## Subsequent official changes
- October 2025: fusion was adjusted so Activity Rating no longer decreases as a consequence of fusion. Treat older min/max rating warnings as outdated.
Source: https://www.oncehuman.game/news/devBlog/20251024/40781_1266818.html
- November 2025: special-scenario passive traits were separated from general passive traits, and female deer/sheep permitted as materials.
Source: https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20251126/40780_1273314.html
- December 2025: fusion outcomes expanded to certain fish and Hydronaut Fish.
Source: https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20251223/40780_1278121.html
- August 2026: developers declined to add extra Fusion Pods because progression pace and balance would change; do not assume arbitrary parallel fusion.
Source: https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20260805/40780_1310273.html

## Cross-reference (community tool; NOT official)
Wikily Fusion Simulator documents a fusion result preview for species odds, trait odds, Skill Rating, Activity Rating, and Stats Profile; materials are consumed. It shows a *five-minute* duration, modifiable by Advanced Recovery Potion. **The five-minute base duration is confirmed by the April 8, 2026 official patch**; the potion interaction still needs current version verification.
https://wikily.gg/once-human/deviation-fusion

## April 2026 timing correction [OFFICIAL]
- The April 8 patch explicitly adjusted Deviation Fusion time to **5 minutes**.
- Source: https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20260408/40780_1295195.html

## Practical strategy
1. Keep desirable high-rating parents for their one permitted parent fusion.
2. Avoid putting a valuable unique Deviation in the material slots unless willing to lose it.
3. Inspect the actual Fusion Pod preview before confirming; odds depend on parents and materials.
4. Do not promise a target trait, species, or identical skill level from a single fusion.
5. Log parent species, skill/activity, existing traits, materials, shown odds and actual outcome to test any proposed 'fusion recipes'.

## Unresolved
Exact chance formulas, trait weighting and whether all variants inherit identically; official sources do not publish a comprehensive universal probability table. Check in-game preview rather than inventing a formula.
