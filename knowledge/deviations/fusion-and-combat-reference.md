# Fusion, Securement, traits and battle Deviations

**Last audit:** 2026-10-31. This page joins older source notes into a usable decision tree. All percentages not supplied by official docs remain UNKNOWN.

## Three different Deviation roles
1. **Territory** — automated resource generation and support (e.g., Growshroom, Wish Box, Rain Man).
2. **Combat** — battle skills/ultimate effects, with battle cooldown and Power costs.
3. **Scenario-specialized** — some game modes use unique combat squads/stat conversion (Deviation: Survive, Capture, Preserve). Their rules cannot automatically be generalized.

## 2025 fusion rules
- Choose **two** parent Deviations. Output type/trait may be inherited from either.
- Parent Deviations **survive** fusion; original rollout permitted each parent to serve as source once.
- **Additional materials are consumed**, potentially including extra Deviations, furniture or certain animals; material choices can influence outcome probabilities and potential traits.
- A later change prevented Activity Rating reduction during fusion.
- Don't promise a single fusion guarantees a 5/5 worker. Check the Fusion Pod's preview and inspect which slot an item occupies.

**Risk table:** parent slots = retained under launch rules; material slots = consumed; resulting species = probabilistic; resulting traits = probabilistic; simulation odds = need cross-check.

## Securement metrics
- **Skill Rating** represents ability rating, but scaling differs by species and ability.
- **Activity Rating** relates to rest/work, not necessarily a universal items/minute increase.
- **Deviant Power** supports actions/abilities; exact threshold varies.
- **Mood** is separate from Power.
- **Environmental preferences** can influence recovery/uptime (species-specific; no universal formula).
- **Passive traits** can affect recovery, skills, capacity or work and can include downsides.

Don't collapse all of these into a fake 'Deviation efficiency percentage'. If someone claims 5/5 is 25% faster than 4/4, ask for a same-species controlled test.

## Modern combat Deviations
December 23, 2025 created:
- **Battle Skill** — active effect with cooldown.
- **Ultimate** — powered ability; effects differ significantly.
- Equip/switch a synchronized combat Deviation via gear backpack.
- **Switching clears Power and resets Battle Skill cooldown**; no free rapid-burst cycling.
- Certain skills and controls differ in RaidZone.
- Dedicated Deviation scenario is explicitly excluded from the standard overhaul.

February 2026 balance work standardized **Weapon, Status and Melee Vulnerability** distinctions. That means a 'vulnerability' effect must specify its type and applicable damage category.

## Cross-system opportunities
- Combat Deviation vulnerability type must be compatible with user's damage source: revolver bullet damage and status-effect damage may benefit differently.
- Wish Box's hourly conversion permits automated waste-to-reward experimentation; it does not guarantee profitable exchange.
- Growshroom + Rain Man + Buzzy Bee can automate planting/watering and mutation attempts, but output requires direct measurement.
- Fusion parent preservation encourages retaining high-quality breeding stock; material consumption means using surplus carefully.
