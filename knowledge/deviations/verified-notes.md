# Verified Deviation and food-decay notes

## Wish Box
Official January 2025 announcement: Wish Box is a territory Deviation with its own storage that transforms deposited items every hour. Only its owner can interact; discarding it destroys stored contents. Official acquisition at launch: Stellar Planula Shop and Morphic Crates.
https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20250115/40780_1207023.html

Player-observed: 8,000 deposited salt, useful rewards including revives and difficult-to-find crafting resources. Exact reward odds unverified.

## Growshroom, Buzzy Bee, Rain Man
Official historical acquisition references from March 2025: Growshroom from chance of mutation while growing mushrooms; Buzzy Bee from world exploration / Silo PHI; Rain Man from Rainfall Reaper. Not guaranteed to cover every 2026 scenario acquisition route.
https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20250312/40780_1218344.html

## Corrosive Agent
Official: toggled on/off with 60 minutes of active duration since September 2, 2026. Official September 28 patch increased spoilage speed substantially without giving an exact multiplier.
https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20260916/40780_1314062.html
https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20260924/40780_1315053.html

Player-observed: one food decay timer changed from 4 hours to 20 minutes, a 12-fold faster timer under the conditions observed. Don't extrapolate to every food or storage arrangement.
