# Securement, ratings, Power and Mood — evidence audit (2026-10-31)

## What the fields are
- **Skill Rating:** a Deviation's capability rating, not synonymous with Energy or Mood.
- **Activity Rating:** associated with rest/recovery and territory operations; high values can matter for uptime.
- **Deviant Power:** consumable resource for active work or combat abilities.
- **Mood:** separate resource affecting recovery and comfort/operation.
- **Securement environment:** each type may benefit from specific nearby objects, light, music, temperature or other conditions.

**Evidence:** game wiki and community testing, NOT a fully up-to-date official equation. Individual stat behavior varies by Deviation class and scenario.

## Important distinction for Shroomazon
Activity Rating does NOT automatically mean a Growshroom is 'better at planting' in every respect. Deviant Power can gate special ability uses, while routine labor may continue. Different Deviations may have separate work thresholds. Measure action counts, seeds consumed and output per hour with controlled comparisons.

## Traits
There are positive and negative passive traits, sometimes with tiers. Trait effects can modify Power maximum, recovery, special production, work patterns, or synergies. Trait names alone do not establish how they combine; record exact in-game tooltip and scenario. The Fandom trait list can provide candidate mechanics, but changes and translations require confirmation.
https://once-human.fandom.com/wiki/Deviation/traits

## Research gaps
- No broadly validated current equation converting Activity Rating to exact Power/minute across every Deviation.
- No universal scaling equation translating Skill Rating to item/hour for work Deviations.
- No verified planter limit per Growshroom or Rain Man.
- Securement unit capacity varies by scenario/phase and progression; older guides saying 5/10/20 should not be applied blindly.

## Measurement protocol
A/B test identical Deviation type with matched traits and surroundings, isolated Power/Mood and rating changes, timestamps for rest cycles, work cycles and production totals. Avoid measurements crossing offline/online states or server phases.
