# Core mechanics — initial research baseline

## Scenario
- **Official (October 2026):** Isles of Abyss is an ocean-focused scenario; see official launch/update articles in the source index.
- **Research needed:** scenario-specific progression, phase unlocks and Calamity Tower enemy objectives.
- Cosmic Visitor and Snowline Elite Vest were distributed together, but this does **not** prove their optimal DPS pairing.

## Mods
- **Official (January 2026):** New mod progression uses a Mod Field Guide, new mod drops and Mod Dust. Do not assume older mod-enhancement guides still apply.
- **Unresolved:** live handling of legacy mods, Weapon Mod Parts and exact mod level ranges in each activity.

## Blueprints
- **Official (March 2026):** Blueprint acquisition and Starchrom systems were reworked.
- **Observed (October 2026):** A player's monthly counter displayed 66,000/66,000, with exploration 25,825, scenario challenges 25,000, game mode 14,575 and daily activities 600.
- **Unresolved:** contribution of salt cooking to the monthly counter. Correlation is not proof of direct reward.

## Ranching
- **Official (2026):** Beastmaster and ranching have been reworked.
- **Unresolved:** precise Acid Secretion yield, trait inheritance, breeding cooldowns, animal limits.

## Deviations
- **Observed:** Growshroom shown with ratings 5/4, 67 seeds, 67 Yield Boost fertilizer, and 24/100 Deviant Power.
- **Player clarification:** Deviant Power governs its special ability, not necessarily basic farming work.
- **Unresolved:** Fusion Pod inheritance, input consumption and repeatability.

## Industry
- **Observed:** One stove produces two salt every five seconds, with five queued slots and two running slots.
- **Derived, conditional:** 24 salt per minute, 1,440 salt per hour, and about 24m45s for a five-slot queue of 99 salt each, assuming sufficient resources.
- **Observed:** Corrosive Agent reduced one food's decay time from four hours to twenty minutes (12x observed spoilage speed).
- **Unresolved:** pump rates, purifier rates, pipe direction, sprinkler coverage.

## Wish Box
- **Observed:** A player placed 8 × 1,000 salt in a Wish Box and reported receiving helpful revives and scarce crafting materials.
- **Unresolved:** rewards, expected value and processing limits.

## Combat
- **Observed:** Approximately 20,000 damage weakspot hits with a high-star R500 Memento.
- **Important:** A displayed hit is not a sustained DPS test.
- **Unresolved:** exact damage stacking, weakspot/crit formula, weapon triggers, equipment exclusivity and boss resistances.

These records intentionally separate observations, developer statements and open questions. See `research/open-questions.md`.
