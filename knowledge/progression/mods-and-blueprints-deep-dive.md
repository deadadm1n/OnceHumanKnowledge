# Blueprint, Starchrom, mods and calibration — post-overhaul reference

**Scope:** 2026 January and March updates; current-game mechanics should be checked for later exceptions. **Evidence:** released official update notes.

## 1. Modern blueprint lifecycle
1. Earn Starchrom from exploration points, settlement rewards, first-clear dungeons, quests and other sources.
2. Unlock weapon and armor **blueprints directly** from the Blueprint screen.
3. Upgrade blueprint star rating directly with Starchrom. Upgrading a blueprint is different from crafting an item.
4. **Blueprint Conversion** shifts star levels between blueprints of the **same rarity**. The stars are transferred, **not duplicated**.
5. After March 25, blueprint fragments were replaced with equivalent Starchrom; old fragments converted at `Fragment Exp × 1.85`. Stellar Stairway/Asterism were retired.
6. Starchrom has a **monthly acquisition cap** displayed on the Blueprint screen; event, Battle Pass and Collection Level rewards can be exempt.
7. The Wish Machine temporarily ceased blueprint draws on March 25; the announced shift toward activity rewards after April 22 must be verified against the eventual release before specifying today's rewards.

### Don't mix these currencies
A player's *spendable Starchrom*, *monthly earned count*, and *remaining monthly earning allowance* answer different questions. A full 66,000/66,000 counter doesn't mean they possess 66,000 spendable currency.

## 2. Mod Field Guide
January 21, 2026 shifted progression toward:
- A Mod Field Guide that records obtained mods.
- Fixed subattributes and Shiny Mods.
- Acquisition through dungeons, gameplay modes, and Mod Dust.
- Compatibility for existing legacy mods with separate rules.
- Old parts and reroll/enhancement tutorials must not be applied to the new system without explicit legacy identification.

**Practical workflow:** compare available mod names, levels, effect descriptions and *currently active* attributes (Tech may gate activation in Isles of Abyss). Duplicate lower-level drops can support Mod Dust progression, subject to current UI.

## 3. Calibration vs calibration blueprints
January 21 removed the **manual Gear Workbench weapon calibration** operation and blueprint replacement there. Instead, add a Calibration Blueprint **while crafting**. New calibration blueprints contain style and Attack attributes (up to 50% Attack by rarity) plus condensed subattribute. Previously calibrated weapons retain unaffected stats.

**Warning:** Do not advise taking a crafted item back to a workbench to roll it repeatedly under the old rules.

## 4. Damage and blueprint-star interpretation
Six stars can be a blueprint cap without being proof of best-in-slot damage. Output depends on active mod lines, ammo, enemies, weakspot precision, reloads, gear effects, damage type, and event buffs.
