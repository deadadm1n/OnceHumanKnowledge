# Mods, blueprints, calibration — versioned mechanics

## Mods [OFFICIAL — 2026-01-21]
The Mod system was overhauled in update 2.3.1: Mod Field Guide; fixed subattributes and shiny mods; acquisition from dungeons, game modes and Mod Dust; legacy mod compatibility. Do not recommend old reroll/enhance procedures for new mods. **Unknown:** exact mod/drop level range by current Isles of Abyss Tech tier.

## Calibration [OFFICIAL — 2026-01-21]
The original gear-workbench calibration functionality was removed; new weapons can receive a Calibration Blueprint during *crafting*. New legendary calibration blueprints have style attributes, an Attack bonus (up to +50%), and one aggregated bonus subattribute (Crit Rate, Crit DMG, Elemental DMG or Weakspot DMG). Existing calibrated gear retains unaffected stats. Recommending 'enhance calibration at the bench' without version qualification is incorrect.

## Blueprints and Starchrom [OFFICIAL — 2026-03-25]
- Direct unlock and star upgrading with Starchrom on the Blueprint screen replaces fragmented acquisition.
- Blueprint Conversion can transfer star levels between blueprints **of the same rarity**, taking the stars from the original.
- Exploration points, settlement treasures, first-clear dungeons, tasks and other former fragment rewards grant Starchrom instead.
- Stellar Stairway and Asterism were removed.
- **Monthly** acquisition cap is shown on the Blueprint screen.
- Events, Battle Pass, Collection Level and similar sources are explicitly exempt from the normal monthly cap.

## Player-specific observations
- Screenshot showed 66,000/66,000 Starchrom cap with categorized income: exploration 25,825; scenario challenges 25,000; game mode 14,575; daily 600.
- R500 Memento and four Lonewolf pieces are reported by player as maximum 6-star.
- Do not mistake spendable Starchrom balance for the monthly acquisition counter.
