# Cross-system dependencies and experiments

## 1. Coastal production
Seawater collection -> stoves -> salt -> optional Wish Box deposits -> randomized loot. Purification branch -> clean water -> sprinklers / agriculture -> food and fertilizer -> goat feed and potential Acid byproducts. Hydropower supplies powered facilities.

**Player observations:** stove 2 salt/5 seconds while both parallel queues run; observed salt queue five slots. Salt production at 8 active stoves theoretically 11,520/hour, **if** continuously supplied. The game limit and manual loading are unverified bottlenecks.

## 2. Spoilage
Player measured food expiring in 20 minutes versus normal four hours with Corrosive Agent, implying 12x time rate under tested conditions. Test other foods and agent durability; avoid extrapolating to food stored outside inventory.

## 3. Wish Box
Player deposited 8,000 salt (8 stacks x 1,000) and received revives and scarce materials. The output distribution and cap are unknown. Do not claim a stable exchange ratio, profitability or high-rarity drop chance without logged runs.

## 4. Agriculture
Growshroom automates planting/harvesting; user reported one seed returned per mushroom plant in some cycles. Rain Man water support, Bee mutation assistance and yield-fertilizer seed effects are not quantitatively verified. Record plant count, harvest count, water percentage and seed returns, plus Deviant Power and work cycles.

## 5. Boss damage
6★ Memento weakspot shooter + 4× 6★ Lonewolf + 1 Bastille and at most one key armor (pending direct verification). AWS Bull's Eye marking -> possible Earthly Boots effect -> Memento shooting. Test marks and buff persistences and the net loss from switching weapons. Isles of Abyss Tech level may disable some mod subattributes.

## Interaction map
- Tech -> craftable weapon rarity and active mod attributes -> DPS ceiling
- Boss weakspot visibility -> Fast Gunner uptime and sniper effectiveness
- Armor slots -> legal key armor + set bonus combinations
- Enemy multiparts -> Shrapnel real-world damage, not a static multiplier
- Food crops -> buffs -> boss combat
- Ship components -> Calamity Tower performance
- Animal feeds -> Energy -> breeding and byproducts

**Rule:** Every edge labeled as observed/hypothesis until quantified by a reproducible test.
