# Animal ranching and Beastmaster — September 2026

## Confirmed
**September 16 class/ranching overhaul** changed animal Energy, riding and summons.
- At 80–100% Energy animals efficiently generate byproducts and can breed; at 20–80% normal production, cannot breed; at 0–20% production/growth pause.
- Mixed Meat, Grain and Herb Feeds raise Energy.
- Offline food comes from troughs, **not dropped food**.
- Wild map resource filter can display animal locations.
- Adult animals with >20% Energy can be summoned as items; 10-second summon cooldown.
- Beastmasters may saddle and mount Deer, Boar, Bear and Wild Buffalo and activate their mount abilities; non-Beastmasters can ride eligible saddled animals but do not get special mount abilities.
- Weapons and consumables can be used while mounted.
- September 28 update confirmed 3 simultaneous animal summons for Beastmasters, 1 for other classes, and one Beast Whistle can command multiple animals.

## IMPORTANT capacity distinction
The **summoned animal limit is not the same as the number of ranch animals allowed at base**. Earlier assistant replies mixed these concepts. Do not assert a territory ranch cap without a newer authoritative source or screenshot.
